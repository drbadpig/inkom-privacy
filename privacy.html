Inkom — Privacy Policy

Last updated: 2026-11-16

Inkom is a personal finance app. Your accounts, transactions, categories and attachments stay on your phone. We do not run servers that receive your data, and we do not collect, sell or share personal information.

What is stored on your phone

Everything you enter: accounts, balances, transactions, notes, categories, recurring payments, debts, settings and the photos you attach. It is saved only in the app’s storage on your device. Deleting the app deletes it.

What leaves your phone

Only a request for the day’s exchange rates, sent to open.er-api.com (ExchangeRate-API) so that totals in different currencies can be converted. The request contains no information about you or your data; like any internet request it reveals your IP address to that service. Nothing else is sent anywhere.

No accounts, analytics or ads

The app has no sign-up, no analytics or tracking tools, no advertising and no third-party SDKs that collect data.

Permissions

Camera and photos: only to attach a picture of a receipt to a transaction, when you choose to. Face ID / fingerprint: only to unlock the app; the check happens in your phone’s system and the app never sees your biometric data. Notifications: reminders scheduled on the phone itself.

Backups and exports

Backups and CSV exports are created only when you ask for them and are handed to the place you choose in the share sheet (for example Files, iCloud Drive, Google Drive or e-mail). From then on that service’s own privacy terms apply.

Feedback

If you write to us through Feedback, we receive the e-mail you send, including your address and anything you include. We use it only to reply and to improve the app, and delete it on request.

Children

The app is not directed at children under 13 and does not knowingly collect data from anyone.

Changes

If this policy changes, the new version will be published at the same address and in the app, with a new date.
